Don't waste time setting up a smartphone that doesn't work. A cautionary tale.

<!--more-->

After seven or so years of daily use, my Pixel 3a's battery has pretty badly degraded. A few months, ago, I decided it was finally time to replace it.

As soon as I got the replacement, a Pixel 10, I started setting up apps and messing with settings right away. I figured that until all this setup and transition stuff was complete I would just keep my SIM card in my old Pixel 3a. That way, I'd move from a fully set up Pixel 3a to a fully set up Pixel 10, with no awkward transition in between.

Big mistake. A few days after, I decided it was time to move the SIM card over, and for a few hours, that worked just fine in my Pixel 10. Then I restarted the phone for an update and it couldn't pick up the network anymore. The phone just would not acknowledge the existence of my carrier's network. I just spent all this time getting comfortable with this new phone only to realize later that it had a pretty serious problem.

It took a visit to the carrier's store and a session with Google's live support to figure this problem out.

If I had moved my SIM card over first thing, I wouldn't have wasted all that time setting up a faulty phone. I would have noticed the issue right away.
